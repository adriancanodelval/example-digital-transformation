# Environment Strategy

## Development

Purpose:

Workspace used by developers.

Characteristics:

- Frequent changes
- Experiments allowed
- Not business critical

---

## Test

Purpose:

Validate developments.

Characteristics:

- Business validation
- User acceptance testing
- Data quality validation

---

## Production

Purpose:

Official environment used by the business.

Characteristics:

- Controlled deployments
- Stable datasets
- Certified reports
