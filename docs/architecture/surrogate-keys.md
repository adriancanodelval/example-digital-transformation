# Natural Key vs Surrogate Key

## Natural Key

A business key coming from the source system.

Examples:

EmployeeID = 123
CustomerID = C1001
ProductID = P500

Natural keys can change and are controlled by the source system.

---

## Surrogate Key

A technical key generated by the Data Warehouse.

Examples:

EmployeeKey = 1
CustomerKey = 10
ProductKey = 100

Surrogate keys are controlled by the Data Warehouse and never reused.

---

## Why use surrogate keys?

- Decouple the warehouse from source systems
- Enable Slowly Changing Dimensions
- Maintain historical records
- Improve performance

---

## Example

Source system:

EmployeeID = 123

Warehouse:

EmployeeKey = 25
EmployeeID = 123
