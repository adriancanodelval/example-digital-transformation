Manage operational business processes.

Entities:

- Orders
- Order Lines
- Products
- Inventory
- Suppliers
- Invoices

## CRM

Main purpose:

Manage customer relationships.

Entities:

- Customers
- Leads
- Opportunities
- Activities

Main purpose:

Manage employee information.

Entities:

- Employees
- Positions
- Departments
- Contracts
